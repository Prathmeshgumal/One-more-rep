Six Directions

The same four screens drawn six ways. Each direction commits to its own palette, typefaces, corner radius, component size and density — not a recolour of the others. Four screens is enough to judge: they cover big numerals, a dense data table, list rhythm, and the primary button. Whichever you pick gets applied to all twenty.

01

Ledger

current

A training log book. Cool graph-paper ground, plate blue, hairline rules, mono for the columns. Quiet and precise — the numbers are the loudest thing on screen.

Archivo + Plex Monoradius 13light
02

Iron

The weight room itself. Near-black with cast-iron greys, heated-metal orange, and Anton set in tight uppercase slabs. Square corners, dense spacing, numbers that read from arm's length on a rack.

Anton + Barlowradius 3dark
03

Clinic

Calm and clinical. Cool mint ground, one confident green, deeply rounded cards and pill buttons. The most spacious of the six — big touch targets, soft edges, nothing shouting.

Plus Jakarta Sansradius 22light
04

Almanac

A printed training diary. Warm paper, Fraunces in its wonky optical cut, deep forest green, zero radius and continuous rules instead of floating cards. Rows sit flush against each other like a ledger page.

Fraunces + Karlaradius 0light
05

Terminal

Quantified-self, no decoration. Monospace everywhere, phosphor green on near-black, square corners, bracketed labels. The densest of the six — maximum data per screen, minimum ornament.

JetBrains Monoradius 0dark
06

Pop

Loud and friendly. Warm off-white, a vivid coral that carries the whole page, Bricolage Grotesque cranked to its display optical size. The biggest type and the largest buttons of the six.

Bricolage + DM Sansradius 19light
