Training Ledger

Eighteen screens for the workout tracker, drawn before any code. The design treats the app as what it actually is — a training log, not a fitness dashboard. Targets render as if pre-printed on the form; what you actually lifted is written over them in ink. That one idea carries Today, Workout, History, and Exercise History.
